What the scanner tests

Every problem comes with a fix, and you see what passed and failed under WCAG 2.2 AA, ADA, GIGW 3.0, and SESMag. Anything only a person can judge becomes a task for your Design and Engineering teams.

  • Page check

    Page check

    Finds common problems, like hard-to-read text, missing labels, and pages that break on small screens or with bigger text. Chrome’s built-in AI also checks image text and links.

  • Keyboard

    Keyboard

    Uses only the keyboard to reach and use everything: links, buttons, menus, tabs, and pop-ups. It checks that focus is easy to see and never gets stuck.

  • Screen reader

    Screen reader

    Shows what a screen reader reads out, in order, so you know what blind and low-vision users will hear.

Test more than the page

A page check only sees what’s on screen when the page opens. But most problems people run into are one click away, in a menu, a pop-up or a form. Choose how deep the scanner goes.

  • Safe mode

    Read-only

    Catch the problems hiding in menus and pop-ups before your users do. Run it on any site, even live production, with zero risk to your data.

    What it finds

    • Buttons and menu items with no name, inside menus and pop-ups
    • Pop-ups that don’t move keyboard focus into them
    • Keyboard focus that escapes an open pop-up
    • Sub-menus that only open on mouse hover
    • Low contrast inside dropdowns and panels

  • Deep mode

    Interactive

    Know your forms work for everyone. Find the silent, confusing and colour-only errors that stop people from finishing a sign-up or an application.

    What it finds

    • Errors shown only in red, with no words
    • Errors that don’t say how to fix them
    • Errors and “Saved” messages screen readers never hear
    • Keyboard focus lost after a form closes
    • Problems on every screen it reaches, with the clicks that led there

  • AI review

    On-device AI

    Get a human reviewer’s judgement for free. Spot meaningless alt text, vague links and unclear labels, with the fix ready to paste.

    What it finds

    • Alt text like “image1.png” or “photo” that says nothing
    • Decorative images that screen readers still read out
    • “Click here” and “Read more” links
    • Headings that don’t describe what’s under them
    • Page titles that don’t say where you are

Checklists for your team

Check these while you build, design, and write. Each item shows the standards it supports.

  • Code checks for structure, images, forms, keyboard and focus, ARIA, and performance.

  • Design checks for colour and contrast, text and layout, states, forms, and navigation.

  • Writing checks for plain language, images, documents, GIGW pages, and the accessibility statement.
